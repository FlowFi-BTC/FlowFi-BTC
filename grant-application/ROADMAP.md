# FlowFi BTC — Roadmap

This roadmap describes where FlowFi BTC could go **after** this grant, if the single pilot proves the mechanism. None of the phases below are funded or promised by this application — see `MILESTONE_PLAN.md` for what this grant actually delivers.

---

## Phase 1 — This Grant: Single-Counterparty Proof

One verified business, one real sBTC provider, one receivable, resolved on-chain through `registry.clar` and `escrow.clar`. The goal is proving the mechanism works and is honestly disclosed — not scale.

---

## Phase 2 — Permissioned Multi-Receivable Pilot (Future, Not Funded Here)

A small, known set of additional businesses and providers — still not open to the public — to test the registry and verification flow at slightly larger scale before any pooling logic is considered. This phase would formalize whichever verification path (manual review or third-party KYB) proved more practical in Phase 1.

---

## Phase 3 — Pluggable Verification Layer (Future)

Multiple verification sources (manual review, Persona or another KYB provider, business registry attestations, debtor confirmation) recorded through the same on-chain schema already designed in `registry.clar`. Verification remains **required** for receivable registration at every phase — this roadmap does not include removing that gate, only diversifying and strengthening how it's satisfied.

---

## Phase 4 — Consumer-Accessible Capital Layer (Future, Requires Legal Structuring)

sBTC holders deploying smaller amounts across multiple verified receivables. This phase is explicitly fenced off: it would require proper legal structuring — likely securities- or crowdfunding-adjacent review — before any build begins. It is named here to show the long-term direction, not to claim it as a near-term deliverable.

---

## What This Roadmap Deliberately Does Not Include

- A fiat/sBTC exchange or P2P conversion service built by this project
- Removing or weakening the verification requirement in the name of "permissionless" design
- Any public solicitation of capital before appropriate legal review
- Promising Phase 2–4 as part of this grant's deliverables

---

## Why the Roadmap Is Written Down at All

Documenting the long-term vision here — rather than building toward it prematurely — lets reviewers see the full thinking behind FlowFi BTC while keeping the actual grant deliverable narrow, provable, and honestly scoped. Any expansion beyond Phase 1 is a future, separate funding and planning conversation.
